# Mr Bands, from the model sheet (web/3d/ref/mr-bands-sheet.png, Zach 2026-09-20)

The sheet supersedes the single drawing (mr-bands-full.png) wherever they differ. Read from it:

- HAT: a tall top hat, the crown ~1.2x the face height, the brim moderately wide with a slight upward curl at the sides.
  The ORANGE IS A VERTICAL STRIPE about a quarter of the crown's width: it starts at the brim at the front centre,
  runs straight up the crown, across the top (the top view shows it front to back) and down the back. It is NOT a band
  round the crown, and there is no bow or knot. The brim's underside is plain black.
- SHADES: the 8-bit "deal with it" glasses: two pixel lenses with a thin bridge and a top bar, arms back to the ears.
- HEAD: a round, jowly face, small nose, rosy cheeks; big white handlebar moustache with upturned tips; the mouth a
  laughing crescent under it. White hair above and behind the EARS, which are VISIBLE from the side and 3/4 views.
- COAT: a long black frock/overcoat to just below the knee, worn open. NOTCH lapels (not peak), a turned-down collar,
  a breast pocket with a white three-point pocket square. Sleeves with TURNED-BACK CUFFS (a darker band) over the white
  shirt cuff. Flap pockets at hip level. At the back: a HALF-BELT at the waist with two buttons, and a centre vent below
  it. From the side the coat hangs straight; the belly pushes the front open.
- WAISTCOAT: dark, five buttons down the front, a pointed hem; a WATCH CHAIN swags from a middle button to the
  waistcoat pocket, where the POCKET WATCH sits (the watch is at the chain's end, in the pocket, not hanging loose).
- SHIRT and TIE: white shirt with a winged collar; an ORANGE butterfly BOW TIE with a knot.
- TROUSERS: black with fine white PINSTRIPES, straight, breaking over the shoe, with a shallow TURN-UP at the hem (a band
  about a fifth of the leg's width, flush with the leg; the front, back and left-side views all draw it).
- SHOES: black CAP-TOE Oxfords, laced, a visible welt and sole edge, a stacked heel; polished.
- CANE: a straight black shaft with a round decorated (pewter) KNOB and a collar under it; a ferrule at the tip.
- CIGAR: brown, a lighter band near the mouth end, a lit tip with grey ash and smoke.
- POSE: cane in his left hand planted a little forward and wide; cigar raised in the right hand beside the cheek;
  a jaunty stance, one foot a little ahead. Stout: the belly is the widest point, the shoulders slope.
