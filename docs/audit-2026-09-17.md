Asked for by Zach: "go over our architecture and logic and run an audit, look for improvements and
optimize upon approval." Nothing in this document has been changed in the code. Section 4 is the list

Facts were measured on the running system at 14:10–14:20Z. The code review in section 3 was done by an

## 1. The architecture as it runs today

Two launchd services on Zach's Mac, one wallet, one Helius RPC.

**The desk** (`com.bands.mrbands.live` → `npm run live` → preflight, then `src/index.ts`), one cycle every 300 s:

1. Screen every 30 min: every Meteora DLMM pool on chain through Helius, the shortlist enriched from DexScreener (volume, market cap, age), scored 0–100.
2. Hot watch, in process, every 120 s: the board's top pools through DexScreener.
3. Memecoin history reads through Helius (signatures), four pools a cycle, cached a day.
4. `pickPools`: held pools, the operator's exit list, hot picks, then board candidates that clear the score floor and the memecoin floors, ordered by the venue's 24h fee on depth, up to `MAX_ACTIVE_POOLS`.
5. Observe each pool: snapshot and positions through Helius. Every worked pool goes on the scout's watch list.
6. Seat check on held seats (fade rule), consolidation, then per pool in `runPool`: engine directive (FLATTEN > STOP > EXPIRE > ROTATE > COLLECT), else an approved proposal, else `decide` (the model when a key exists, the desk policy otherwise) → `evaluate` (the guards) → `execute` (Meteora SDK, Jupiter swaps on Meteora-only routes) → ledger rows → the lesson on a close → state → journal entry.
7. Marks: equity, the circuit and portfolio breakers. The tuner. The skim. Auto-deploy of both sites, at most every 30 min.

**The flow scout** (`com.bands.mrbands.flow` → `src/scripts/flow.ts`): every 5 s, new signatures for each watched pool, every non-failed transaction fetched and its DLMM swap events decoded, rolling windows from 1 min to 4 h (fees, volume, our bins' share, the bins touched), written to `flow.json`; every swap appended to `flow-events.jsonl`.

**Decision principle:** the model or the policy proposes, the guards decide. Limits live in code and config, never in a prompt. The tuner (new today) moves only the band width multiple.

**Sites:** one Vite app, two Vercel projects (bands.finance, mr-bands-live), static snapshots of the journal pushed by the desk.

**State on disk** (`data-mainnet/`): `decisions.jsonl`, `latest.json`, `ledger.jsonl`, `state.json`, `engine-state.json`, `equity.jsonl`, `flow.json`, `flow-events.jsonl`, `flow-watch.json`, `lessons.jsonl`, `tuning.json`, `screen.json`, `hot.json`. Config: `ops/live.env` (about 60 settings), secrets in `.env`, `DRY_RUN=false` only in the live plist.

Size: `src/index.ts` 2,069 lines, `policy.ts` 1,364, `executor.ts` 724; 22 test suites, all green.

## 2. Findings, by money at risk

### A. Operations

**A1. The desk runs on a laptop set to sleep after one minute. HIGH.**
`pmset` shows `sleep 1`; sleep is held off right now only by incidental assertions (Chrome, a stray `caffeinate`). With the lid closed or those gone, cycles stop: no stops, no exits, live positions unmanaged. None of the plists holds a wake assertion.

**A2. Sixty commits exist only on this machine. HIGH, cheap.**
`main` is 60 ahead of `origin/main`. Everything built today is one disk failure from gone.

**A3. Every code change needs a hand-timed restart. MEDIUM.**
About twelve restarts today, each placed in the idle window by watching the log. One mistimed restart can cut a close from its liquidation swap.

**A4. Secrets on the laptop; one key string is doing two jobs. MEDIUM.**
The Helius key in `.env` is the same string Zach gave earlier as the ClawPump agent id. One of the two is wrong or both are exposed as one.

**A5. Logs and event files grow without rotation. LOW.**
`flow-events.jsonl` grows about 27 MB a day at today's flow.

### B. Risk between and inside cycles

**B1. Stops and exits are checked once every 300 s. HIGH for a memecoin book.**
baton moved six bins (6%) in six minutes today; on paper day one a token fell 86% inside one interval and took 18.6 SOL from one band. The scout already knows the price every five seconds; the desk does not look at it between cycles.

**B2. A bid-only band that the price goes through sits as the token for about 25 minutes before the policy closes it. MEDIUM-HIGH.**
baton's first seat: 7.50 SOL in, 7.02 back, 0.48 SOL of fees, net about zero. Day one on paper, through-band closes went from 5 SOL down at the crossing to 13 SOL down by the close. Two known remedies, neither chosen yet: exit on crossing, or straddles for memecoin pools.

**B3. Fee tokens claimed to the wallet are never sold. MEDIUM.**
A close liquidates only what the band hands back (`executor.ts:581–613`). Claims paid in the base token stay in the wallet: 1,270 ALLINU (0.21 SOL) now, outside the exposure count, growing with every claim.

**B4. Seat size ignores the token's volatility and our share of the side. MEDIUM.**
Every seat is 7.5 SOL. In baton's $62k pool that was 36% of the bid side.

### C. Decision quality

**C1. Board candidates are ordered by the venue's 24h fee on depth, not by measured seat yield. MEDIUM.**
The scout confirms before entry but does not rank, and the yield ranking, rotation and consolidation built this morning serve only the stock lane, which is off.

**C2. The seat check assumes the band is in range all the time. MEDIUM.**
ALLINU read 640%/day while its bid band sat idle below the price, earning nothing. A busy pool with an out-of-range band can never read as faded, and it is not re-laid until the out-of-range timer runs out.

**C3. There is no markout. MEDIUM, high value.**
The desk measures fees, not whether its fills were adverse. For memecoins that is the edge. The scout has every swap and our band; the price N minutes after each fill through our bins is computable per seat and belongs in the lessons.

**C4. No model in the loop. LOW-MEDIUM.**
No Anthropic key, so the desk policy decides everything and the daily reflection does not run.

**C5. Paper accounting leftovers. LOW.**
The paper mark's double 0.5 haircut and the modelled fees inside the paper history's "fees earned" are still there. Not live money.

### D. Cost and efficiency

**D1. The scout fetches every successful transaction on every watched pool. HIGH for availability.**
41 swaps a minute decoded at 14:15Z across 12 pools, which is at least 59,000 `getTransaction` calls a day before counting the non-swap transactions it fetches and discards; ALLINU alone has carried 250 transactions a minute. On a Helius plan with a monthly credit cap this breaks first, and the desk's entry gate (`POLICY_REQUIRE_FLOW`) then holds every open.
The pool's own account carries what the ranking needs: the active bin (price travel, exactly) and the protocol fee counters (fee pace, exactly). One account read per pool per poll is two orders of magnitude cheaper. Per-transaction decoding is only needed for the pools we hold (our bins' share, markouts).

**D2. `src/index.ts` is 2,069 lines and every change today went through it. MEDIUM.**
Picker, ranking, the cycle, seat checks, lessons hooks and deploy in one file; defects today clustered there (pass ordering, stale exposure, picker against policy).

**D3. About 60 settings, defaults scattered across modules, no validated effective config. LOW-MEDIUM.**
A typo in `ops/live.env` falls back silently; the 4% width cap that priced baton out was such a default.

**D4. The desk rebuilds and pushes both sites every 30 minutes. LOW.**
Fifteen pushes today. Inside Vercel's limits; the platform site rarely changes.

### E. The learning loop (new today)

**E1. One knob, and lessons only at a close. MEDIUM.**
With two seats, five lessons take most of a day. Interim readings of open seats (hourly) would teach faster. Further knobs (rotation factor, entry floors, seat size) are not built.

**E2. Tuning applies without review. Your call.**
Bounded and logged, but you may want the first changes as proposals you approve.

## 3. Code review of today's changes

An independent reviewer read `git diff cad1311..HEAD` (today's live-money code) without my conclusions.
It found nothing that breaches the exposure limits or corrupts state: exposure stays bounded at two
seats of 7.5 SOL, units are consistent throughout, and the new state maps persist correctly. It did find
defects that make the desk act on partial or wrong data. I checked the two most consequential against the
journal, the state file and the logs; those are marked "verified".

### Defects, most severe first

**R1. A scout reading of a few minutes counts as an hour. Verified, live.**
`index.ts:1270` attaches the scout's reading whenever coverage is not null, however short, and the policy
takes `range60mBins` and `fees60m x 24` as the hour's figures with no coverage check (`policy.ts:263`, `437`;
`scouts/flow.ts:308`). The busiest pools hit the 8,000-signature backfill cap, so their first reading is
always under an hour. ALLINU/SOL was opened at 13:26Z on **18 minutes** of coverage (journal:
`coveredMin 18`, four-hour pace null). Under the new width rule a short window understates the hour's travel,
which is the baton mistake through another door.

**R2. The exit list does not stop a listed pool being seated again. Live, masked.**
`ROTATE_OUT_POOLS` only turns a held band into a rotation (`index.ts:697`); nothing excludes the pool from
the picker, the rotation has no minimum age, and both listed pools (closed at 12:34Z and 12:39Z) are still
listed. Either could be seated and closed the next cycle, paying rent, fees and two swaps each pass.
Today they are kept out only by the volume and market-cap floors and by R9.

**R3. The tuner re-applies the same lessons after every gap. Live.**
It reads every lesson of the last 24 h, not those since its last change (`lessons.ts:189`; `index.ts:1925`).
Five lessons that justify one step would buy a second step six hours later and a third at twelve, with no new
evidence and no look at whether the first step helped.

**R4. The seat check's prediction is never saved. Verified.**
It is written on a copy of state that was already saved and is then overwritten (`index.ts:1866` against
`1819`, `1240`). `state.json` shows `predictedYieldPct: null` for GP/SOL after several seat checks, so every
lesson will lack its prediction.

**R5. The scout loses swaps silently while claiming full coverage.**
Incremental polls read one page of 100 signatures (`scripts/flow.ts:144`; the ten-page allowance is dead
code), failed transaction fetches are swallowed with no retry or count (`:111`), and each new pool's
80-page backfill stalls the single loop for 1.5 to 2.5 minutes. `flow.log` holds **263 rate-limit (429)
lines today**. Two backfills in one pass can also make `flow.json` older than three minutes, which empties
the desk's view and holds every open that cycle. The error understates flow, which is conservative for entry.

**R6. An off-board hot pick can never open under the flow gate, yet takes a seat every cycle.**
`screenContext` is null for a pool on the hot list but off the board, so the reading is never attached and
the policy holds "waiting for the scout" forever (`index.ts:1102`, `1270`; `policy.ts:992`). Hot picks are
taken first, so the seat is wasted while the pool stays hot.

**R7. Re-sizing a held straddle counts our own liquidity as the pool's depth. Dormant (no straddle held).**
The depth cap and the grow test read a snapshot that includes our band (`policy.ts:547`, `1113`), so
successive re-lays can walk past the 50% share rule; the same inflation feeds the consolidation cap, and it
understates a held seat's share against candidates (`seatYield.ts:61`).

**R8. A close inside a failed execution skips the lesson and the bookkeeping. Low.**
A failed liquidation leg or a rebalance whose re-open fails writes no lesson (the most expensive exits),
leaks the band's meta, and sets no sit-out (`index.ts:1461`, `1492`). The end reason is taken from the
policy's headline, not the guard's, so a guard stop reads as "close". Consecutive seats in one pool can share
each other's swap rows in the net figure (`lessons.ts:100`).

**R9. Smaller items.**
The picker's score floor is `>=` and the policy's is `>`, so a pool scoring exactly 20 is picked and refused
every cycle. `flowWatch.clear()` wipes the stock lane's ranked candidates before they reach the scout, so
with that lane on no new stock candidate could ever be seated (dormant). A partly covered hour is ranked as
a full hour for held seats (dormant). The fade streak is never cleared when a seat closes, the fade check is
skipped in any cycle that already has a rotation, and `SEAT_FADE_FACTOR=0` cannot switch it off.

### The fade rule and idle bands (my question to the reviewer, then verified further)

An out-of-range band never reads as faded: the seat check models a band centred on the current price, so an
idle band in a busy pool reads healthy. GP/SOL read 359%/day in range and **557%/day while idle** earning
nothing. The stored prediction is fiction while a band is out of range.

Checking that led to a larger leak: **an idle bid band (price above it, all SOL) waits three times the
out-of-range minimum before it follows the price up** (`policy.ts:853`, `IDLE_MULTIPLE = 3`), which is 30 to
75 minutes, although re-laying an all-SOL band needs no swap and costs only network fees. Time in range
today from the desk's own counters: ALLINU 3 of 7 cycles, GP 1 of 5. GP was laid 6 bins wide from a quiet
hour (6 bins) while its four-hour travel was 13, and has sat idle since about 14:26Z.

### Design weaknesses worth acting on

- **W1. The model would bypass today's entry rules. HIGH, because a key is about to be added.** Flow-first, the
  swap-impact cap, the width rule and the grow limits live in the policy. With `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set the model
  proposes instead, and the guards enforce none of them.
- **W2. The fade rule treats zero as truth.** A scout that is alive but cannot decode (an IDL change, a pool
  erroring each poll) decays the four-hour pace to zero and the desk would rotate every seat out within about
  four hours. Nothing separates "quiet" from "unreadable".
- **W3. Non-critical steps run before positions are managed each cycle,** on the same rate-limited key: up to
  48 signature calls for history and 7.5 s of leftover pacing sleeps.
- **W4. Lessons carry no mode.** A rehearsal (dry-run, same data folder) could write lessons, delete live
  band meta and step the live knob.
- **W5. The tuner's signal is confounded.** Wide bands exit by stop before "through-band", and stops are not
  counted; the knob is learned from memecoin seats but also widens stock straddles; once the tuning file holds
  a value, `POLICY_VOL_MULTIPLE` in the env is silently ignored.
- **W6. One floor, two yardsticks.** Entry uses `seatEarnings` (halved, side-depth share); the seat check and
  the ranking use `seatYield` (no in-range factor, per-bin share). The numbers are not comparable.

### `ops/live.env` against the code

- `ROTATE_OUT_POOLS` is stale (R2). Two comments are stale ("no memecoins", "2x better").
- `BOOK=all` is not memecoin-only: a verified stock pool on the board is still pickable and would be straddled
  unhedged.
- `POLICY_MIN_COVER_PCT` is still the 0.15% default: a quiet-hour reading of zero or one bin would put a
  7.5 SOL bid into one or two bins.
- `MEME_MIN_HISTORY_DAYS=3` is inert for the busy pools this book targets; the 72-hour age rule does the work.
- `SWAP_DEXES="Meteora DLMM"` and the default 50 bps swap slippage are stock-book choices in a book whose main
  exit is selling a falling memecoin. A failed liquidation leaves the token in the wallet and nothing sells it later.
