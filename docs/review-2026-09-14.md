# Code review, 2026-09-14

Scope: every commit since the redesign landed (`4c82a4a~1..HEAD`, 22 commits today) plus the
working tree. Method: nine independent finder passes (line scan ×3, removed-behaviour audit,
cross-file trace, reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude), 54 raw candidates deduplicated
to 41, each verified by a separate reader against the code. 39 confirmed, 2 plausible, 1 refuted
(the sibling-cache TTL bug, fixed in the launch lane before the verifier reached it). One more
defect was found live on the running desk during the review and reproduced.

Verdict counts do not mean the desk is broken. It runs, journals, rebalances and reconciles.
They mean the money path has not been read by anyone but the people who wrote it until now.
